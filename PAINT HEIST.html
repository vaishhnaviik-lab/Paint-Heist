How to Play 🐱🐭

  • 🐭The rat stole all 8 colors! Chase him through the garden, village, forest and rainbow world.
  • ❓When you catch up, the rat asks a question. Pick the right answer to win a color.
  • 🔁Wrong answer? No worries. The rat just laughs and you can try again.
  • 🎨Each color you win paints the drawing. Tap a color, then tap the drawing to paint your own way.
  • ⭐Get a star for every answer you nail on the first try and for every finished drawing.
